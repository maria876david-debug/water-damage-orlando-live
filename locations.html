Systematic Protocol

5-Step Restoration Deployment

From emergency call intake to final dry-out verification, matched contractors follow industry-standard mitigation protocols.

1
Immediate Intake

Phone Triage & Dispatch Connection

You speak with our intake team to assess the water damage category and get connected with an independent local emergency crew dispatched with typical arrival within 45 to 60 minutes*.

2
Diagnostic Mapping

Moisture Inspection & Thermal Imaging

Technicians employ FLIR infrared cameras and moisture meters to map moisture boundaries behind baseboards, ceilings, crawlspaces, and subfloors.

3
Active Extraction

High-Volume Water Removal

Truck-mounted extraction units and weighted subfloor extractors remove standing water to prevent deep saturation into building framing.

4
Structural Drying

LGR Dehumidification & Air Movement

Low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers paired with centrifugal air movers actively pull evaporated moisture from structural materials, targeting pre-loss dry standards.

5
Insurance Documentation

Xactimate Billing & Carrier Support

Contractors maintain detailed moisture logs, psychrometric records, and line-item estimates compatible with major homeowners insurance adjusters.
