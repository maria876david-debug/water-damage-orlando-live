Company History & Mission

Our Story: Built on Central Florida Building Science

In 2008, following severe tropical weather cycles across Central Florida, Having witnessed out-of-state storm chasers and unqualified handymen leave saturated subfloors to fester behind fresh drywall—creating severe toxic black mold infestations within 48 hours—it was clear that Central Florida needed an engineering-first restoration contractor.

Florida’s sub-tropical climate, characterized by an average relative humidity of 74% and high dew points, renders standard household fans completely useless during a water loss. Our mission was established on strict psychrometric science: measuring vapor pressure differentials, deploying high-capacity low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers, and following the IICRC S500 Standard for Professional Water Damage Restoration to the letter.

Over the past 18+ years, we have restored more than 4,200 residential and commercial properties throughout Orange County without using third-party franchise call centers. When you call us, you speak directly with our local restoration coordinators based at 100 N Orange Ave in Downtown Orlando.

Scientific Drying Methodology

Our 5-Stage Psychrometric Restoration Process

Proper water damage restoration requires balancing temperature, air velocity, and specific humidity to extract moisture bound inside porous structural materials. Here is how our certified technicians manage every job from start to finish.

01

Emergency Phone Intake and Rapid Dispatch

When you call (407) 557-9924, our dispatch coordinator collects key details about the water source, safety hazards (such as electrical exposure), and building layout. We provide immediate safety instructions and dispatch a fully equipped service vehicle with extraction units and drying gear.

02

Thermal Moisture Mapping and Damage Perimeter Scope

Water travels along hidden pathways inside wall cavities and underneath baseboards. Our technicians use infrared thermal cameras and non-invasive moisture meters to map moisture perimeters accurately without unnecessary destruction.

03

High-Volume Liquid Extraction

Physical liquid extraction is 500 times faster than evaporation. Using truck mounted and portable vacuum units, we extract standing water from carpets, hardwood floors, tile grout lines, and concrete subfloors before drying equipment is powered on.

04

Controlled Psychrometric Structural Drying

We deploy industrial low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers and high-velocity axial air movers configured to optimize vapor pressure differentials. Our technicians log daily humidity, temperature, and material moisture percentages on digital psychrometric charts.

05

Antimicrobial Treatment and Final Moisture Sign-Off

We apply botanical antimicrobial sanitizers to affected building surfaces to prevent fungal spores from germinating. Once all structural materials meet standard dry equilibrium targets, we generate formal completion documentation for your insurance provider.

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Team & Credentials

Everything property owners need to know about our certifications, state licensing, insurance procedures, and Orange County emergency response times.

Are your water damage technicians following industry best practices?

Yes. Every lead technician at Orlando Rapid Restoration holds active IICRC (Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification) credentials, including Water Damage Restoration (WRT), Applied Structural Drying (ASD), and Applied Microbial Remediation (AMRT).

What licenses does Florida require for water damage restoration and mold remediation?

The State of Florida requires contractors performing mold remediation to carry a Florida Mold Remediator License (DBPR license type MRSR) and state-approved general or building contractor credentials for structural rebuilds. Orlando Rapid Restoration holds FL Mold Remediator Orlando, FL and Certified Building Contractor Orlando, FL.

How quickly can your restoration crew arrive at my property in Orlando?

Our average emergency response time across Orange County is 35 to 45 minutes. We maintain 24/7 rapid dispatch units positioned strategically along the I-4 corridor, SR-408, and SR-417 to reach Downtown, Winter Park, College Park, and Lake Nona quickly.

Do you bill my homeowners insurance directly?

Yes. We bill your insurance carrier directly and document all structural losses, psychrometric drying logs, and moisture meter readings using Xactimate, the industry-standard software used by all major insurance adjusters.
