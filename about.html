Scientific Drying Methodology

Our 5-Stage Psychrometric Restoration Process

Proper water damage restoration requires balancing temperature, air velocity, and specific humidity to extract moisture bound inside porous structural materials. Here is how our certified technicians manage every job from start to finish.

01

Emergency Phone Intake and Rapid Dispatch

When you call (407) 557-9924, our dispatch coordinator collects key details about the water source, safety hazards (such as electrical exposure), and building layout. We provide immediate safety instructions and dispatch a fully equipped service vehicle with extraction units and drying gear.

02

Thermal Moisture Mapping and Damage Perimeter Scope

Water travels along hidden pathways inside wall cavities and underneath baseboards. Our technicians use infrared thermal cameras and non-invasive moisture meters to map moisture perimeters accurately without unnecessary destruction.

03

High-Volume Liquid Extraction

Physical liquid extraction is 500 times faster than evaporation. Using truck mounted and portable vacuum units, we extract standing water from carpets, hardwood floors, tile grout lines, and concrete subfloors before drying equipment is powered on.

04

Controlled Psychrometric Structural Drying

We deploy industrial low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers and high-velocity axial air movers configured to optimize vapor pressure differentials. Our technicians log daily humidity, temperature, and material moisture percentages on digital psychrometric charts.

05

Antimicrobial Treatment and Final Moisture Sign-Off

We apply botanical antimicrobial sanitizers to affected building surfaces to prevent fungal spores from germinating. Once all structural materials meet standard dry equilibrium targets, we generate formal completion documentation for your insurance provider.
