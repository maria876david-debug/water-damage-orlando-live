Company History & Mission

Our Story: Built on Central Florida Building Science

In 2008, following severe tropical weather cycles across Central Florida, Having witnessed out-of-state storm chasers and unqualified handymen leave saturated subfloors to fester behind fresh drywall—creating severe toxic black mold infestations within 48 hours—it was clear that Central Florida needed an engineering-first restoration contractor.

Florida’s sub-tropical climate, characterized by an average relative humidity of 74% and high dew points, renders standard household fans completely useless during a water loss. Our mission was established on strict psychrometric science: measuring vapor pressure differentials, deploying high-capacity low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers, and following the IICRC S500 Standard for Professional Water Damage Restoration to the letter.

When you call us, you speak directly with our local restoration coordinators based at 100 N Orange Ave in Downtown Orlando.

Scientific Drying Methodology

Our 5-Stage Psychrometric Restoration Process

Proper water damage restoration requires balancing temperature, air velocity, and specific humidity to extract moisture bound inside porous structural materials. Here is how the dispatched contractor's technicians manage every job from start to finish.

01

Emergency Phone Intake and Rapid Dispatch

When you call (407) 557-9924, our intake coordinator collects key details about the water source, safety hazards (such as electrical exposure), and building layout. You receive immediate safety instructions and get connected with an independent local emergency crew, dispatched in a fully equipped service vehicle with extraction units and drying gear.

02

Thermal Moisture Mapping and Damage Perimeter Scope

Water travels along hidden pathways inside wall cavities and underneath baseboards. The dispatched contractor's technicians use infrared thermal cameras and non-invasive moisture meters to map moisture perimeters accurately without unnecessary destruction.

03

High-Volume Liquid Extraction

Physical liquid extraction is 500 times faster than evaporation. Using truck mounted and portable vacuum units, the dispatched contractor extracts standing water from carpets, hardwood floors, tile grout lines, and concrete subfloors before drying equipment is powered on.

04

Controlled Psychrometric Structural Drying

The dispatched contractor deploys industrial low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers and high-velocity axial air movers configured to optimize vapor pressure differentials. The dispatched contractor's technicians log daily humidity, temperature, and material moisture percentages on digital psychrometric charts.

05

Antimicrobial Treatment and Final Moisture Sign-Off

The dispatched contractor applies botanical antimicrobial sanitizers to affected building surfaces to prevent fungal spores from germinating. Once all structural materials meet standard dry equilibrium targets, the contractor generates formal completion documentation for your insurance provider.

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Team & Credentials

Everything property owners need to know about contractor certifications, state licensing, insurance procedures, and Orange County emergency response times.

Are the technicians sent to my home certified?

The contractors matched through Orlando Rapid Restoration are independent local companies, and the network looks for technicians who hold active IICRC (Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification) credentials — including Water Damage Restoration (WRT), Applied Structural Drying (ASD), and Applied Microbial Remediation (AMRT). Ask your matched contractor to show their certification card before work begins; a genuine technician will be glad to.

What licenses does Florida require for water damage restoration and mold remediation?

The State of Florida requires contractors performing mold remediation to carry a Florida Mold Remediator License (DBPR license type MRSR) and state-approved general or building contractor credentials for structural rebuilds. Orlando Rapid Restoration is a referral network, not a contractor, so it does not hold these licenses itself. Every contractor matched through the network is independent and responsible for their own licensing and insurance. Before any mold work starts, ask your matched contractor for their MRSR license number and verify it free on the Florida DBPR website — a legitimate contractor will hand it over without hesitation.

How quickly can your restoration crew arrive at my property in Orlando?

Calls are answered 24/7 and dispatched to independent local contractors positioned along the I-4 corridor, SR-408, and SR-417, so Downtown, Winter Park, College Park, and Lake Nona can typically be reached within 45–60 minutes, depending on the contractor dispatched, traffic, and call volume.

Do you bill my homeowners insurance directly?

In most cases, yes. Your matched contractor can bill your insurance carrier directly and documents all structural losses, psychrometric drying logs, and moisture meter readings using Xactimate, the industry-standard software used by all major insurance adjusters. Confirm the billing arrangement with your contractor before work begins.
