Neighborhood Specifics

Why Water Damage in Metro West Presents High Multi-Unit Risk

Metro West was master-planned around rolling hills, golf fairways, and dozens of retention waterways in southwest Orlando. Developed extensively through the late 1980s, 1990s, and 2000s, the area is characterized by dense multi-family condominium communities, apartment complexes, and stucco slab-on-grade homes that create distinct water loss scenarios.

01 / Multi-Family Infrastructure

Upper-Floor Vertical Water Cascades

In multi-story communities such as The Hamptons at MetroWest, Horizons, and Bermuda Dunes, water heaters and washing machine hoses situated on upper floors present significant liability. When a 50-gallon tank fails or a supply line snaps on the third level, hundreds of gallons seep through lightweight gypcrete underlayment, insulation, and drywall into first-floor units below.

Key Risk: Multi-unit water cascade requiring rapid multi-level containment and simultaneous drying.
02 / Climate Control Strain

Attic & Closet HVAC Condensate Floods

Florida's intense humidity forces air conditioning systems in MetroWest homes to run non-stop for nine months of the year, producing 10 to 20 gallons of condensate daily. When algae lines clog narrow PVC drain tubes, overflow pans spill into ceiling cavities, resulting in collapsed drywall ceilings, soaked carpets, and hidden mold behind air handler closets.

Key Risk: Ceiling drywall collapse and undetected mold growth inside interior AC mechanical closets.
03 / Drainage & Slab Mechanics

Golf Course Retention Pond & Slab Backups

Villas bordering the MetroWest Golf Club along Robert Trent Jones Drive and Westpointe Boulevard sit adjacent to engineered retention basins. During torrential storm cycles, high groundwater saturation exerts hydrostatic pressure against monolithic concrete slabs, pushing moisture upward through micro-cracks and flooding ground-floor sliding patio entries.

Key Risk: Hydrostatic foundation slab saturation causing tile adhesive breakdown and efflorescence.
