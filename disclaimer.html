Overview

Orlando Rapid Restoration is a referral and information service. This Disclaimer explains the limits of the information published on this website and of the service we provide, so you know exactly what we do — and what we do not do — before you rely on anything you read here.

1. A Referral Service, Not a Restoration Contractor

Orlando Rapid Restoration connects Orlando-area homeowners and property managers with independent local water damage restoration contractors. We do not ourselves perform water extraction, structural drying, mold remediation, or repair work, and we are not a restoration contractor. When you call us or submit a form on this website, we pass your request to a local contractor, and that contractor performs the on-site work.

2. Cost Figures and Timelines Are Estimates, Not Quotes

The cost ranges, drying timelines, and response-time information published on this website are general estimates based on typical Central Florida water damage jobs. They are provided for information and planning purposes only and are not quotes, bids, or guarantees of price, scope, or arrival time. Every water loss is different: the actual scope, timeline, and price for your property can only be set by the contractor after an on-site inspection, and may differ from the figures shown here.

3. Independent Contractors Are Responsible for Their Work

Each contractor we connect you with is an independent business and is solely responsible for its own work, employees, licensing, and insurance. Orlando Rapid Restoration does not warrant or guarantee the workmanship of any independent contractor. Before work begins, we encourage you to verify the contractor’s license and insurance and to get the agreed scope of work and price in writing.

4. Insurance Information Is General Guidance Only

The insurance-related content on this website is general educational information about how water damage claims commonly work in Florida. It is not insurance, legal, or financial advice, and it does not promise that any claim will be covered. Coverage decisions are made solely by your insurer under your own policy.

5. Emergencies: Call 911 First

If there is any immediate threat to safety — fire, sparking or submerged electrical equipment, a gas smell, serious injury, or rising water endangering occupants — call 911 first. Do not wait for a restoration referral in a life-safety emergency, and only shut off water or power if you can do so safely.

6. Accuracy of Information

We work to keep the information on this website accurate and current, but we make no warranty that it is complete or free of errors. Conditions, pricing, and best practices change over time, and nothing on this site replaces the on-site assessment of the contractor who inspects your property.
