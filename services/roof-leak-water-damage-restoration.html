Roof Leak Water Damage Restoration in Orlando, FL

Emergency roof tarping, insulation removal, and attic dry-out.

A sudden gust of wind rips off several roof shingles during a summer storm, allowing a massive downpour to penetrate your attic space. Water begins trickling through your ceiling fixtures and pooling in your attic insulation, threatening a major structural failure in the ceiling below. You need immediate tarping, extraction, and attic dry-out to prevent widespread structural rot.

What This Service Covers

  • Emergency roof tarping and shrink-wrap stabilization.
  • Wet attic insulation extraction and disposal.
  • Structural drying of damp roof rafters, trusses, and sheathing.
  • Ceiling drywall stabilization and overhead moisture mapping.
  • Broad-spectrum wood sealant and mold-preventative treatment.

Why This Happens in Orlando Homes

Orlando's intense hurricane season and violent summer afternoon storms pack high-velocity winds that easily tear shingles, tile, and flashing from roofs. Florida's high UV index also degrades asphalt shingles over time, causing them to crack and leak.

Our Process for Roof Leak Restoration

We stop active water, clear soaked insulation, and dry out attic wood structures.

1

Emergency Tarping

Our team safely climbs your roof to install a heavy-duty, wind-resistant tarp, stopping the active rain intrusion.

2

Attic Insulation Removal

Saturated blown-in or fiberglass insulation is vacuumed out to expose the damp ceiling joists.

3

Thermal Inspection

We map the moisture path through your ceilings and walls using advanced thermal imaging cameras.

4

Structural Desiccant Drying

High-output air movers are placed in the attic and living spaces to dry the structural wood trusses and drywall.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have direct answers from IICRC certified technicians.

Speak to a professional
How do you locate a roof leak from inside the attic?

Water rarely travels in a straight line, often entering through a roof leak, running down a rafter, and dripping onto your ceiling feet away from the actual roof breach. We use infrared thermal cameras to trace the cold temperature signature of the water path directly to its source.

Should wet attic insulation always be removed?

Yes, wet attic insulation must be removed because it acts as a massive wet blanket trapped over your ceiling. It will compress, lose its R-value, and hold moisture against your ceiling joists for weeks, inevitably leading to wood rot and severe mold growth.

Can a roof leak cause an electrical fire?

Yes, water leaking through your roof can travel along electrical conduit and pool inside ceiling light fixtures or junction boxes. This creates a severe risk of electrical short circuits, sparks, and localized structural fires, requiring you to turn off affected breakers.

How long does a roof tarp last in Orlando's weather?

A professionally installed, heavy-duty roof tarp can last up to ninety days in Orlando's intense sun and wind. This gives you ample time to coordinate with your insurance company and schedule a licensed roofing contractor for permanent repairs.
