Ceiling Leak Water Damage Repair in Orlando, FL

Professional repair and structural drying for leaking ceilings.

A dark, heavy ring slowly expands across your ceiling plaster until a chunk of soggy drywall crashes onto your living room floor. Water begins dripping steadily from the exposed joists onto your furniture below. This structural failure requires immediate support to prevent a total ceiling collapse.

What This Service Covers

  • Sagging drywall stabilization and safe overhead demolition.
  • Inspection of second-story plumbing connections and roof transition zones.
  • Overhead cavity thermal imaging to find hidden pooling water.
  • High-velocity structural drying of ceiling joists and subfloors.
  • Anti-microbial sanitation of overhead insulation and timber.

Why This Happens in Orlando Homes

Intense daily summer thunderstorms dump massive volumes of water that test roof valleys and flashings to their limits. Meanwhile, second-floor air conditioning drain lines frequently clog due to rapid algae growth in Florida's persistent heat, sending overflow water straight through your ceiling drywall.

Our Process for Ceiling Leak Repair

We execute standard professional procedures to protect your interior and framing.

1

Controlled Drainage & Containment

We safely drain any trapped pooling water and shield your flooring and furniture with heavy-duty protective sheeting.

2

Damaged Drywall Removal

Soggy, structural-weak drywall is removed to expose the damp ceiling framing.

3

Deep Overhead Drying

Industrial axial fans are directed into the ceiling cavity to dry out the joists and subflooring overhead.

4

Multi-Surface Sanitizing

An EPA-registered antimicrobial agent is applied to kill mold spores and prevent musty odors in the ceiling void.

Get Expert Advice
How can I tell if a water stain on my ceiling is active?

An active ceiling leak will feel damp to the touch and may expand in size during rainstorms or when second-story plumbing is used. We verify active moisture using non-invasive digital moisture meters, which measure the exact electrical conductivity inside the drywall core to confirm if water is still flowing.

What causes a ceiling to sag after a leak?

Drywall is made of gypsum and paper, which absorb water like a sponge. When saturated, the drywall loses its structural integrity and pulls away from the ceiling screws under its own immense wet weight. A sagging ceiling is extremely dangerous and can collapse at any moment.

Do you repair the source of the ceiling leak?

Yes, we identify whether the leak is originating from a broken pipe, a clogged AC condensate drain line, or a roof compromise. Our team repairs the plumbing and AC line issues immediately to ensure your ceiling remains dry after we finish the drying process.

Can overhead ceiling insulation be saved after a leak?

Saturated fiberglass or cellulose insulation in ceilings must be removed because it loses its insulating properties and acts as a massive reservoir for mold. We safely extract wet insulation, thoroughly dry the joist cavities, and prepare the space for clean, mold-resistant replacement material.
