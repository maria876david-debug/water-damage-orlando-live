Decontamination & Pathogen Remediation • Orlando, FL

Certified Biohazard, Bloodborne Pathogen & Sewage Decontamination

Biological hazards introduce severe, immediate health risks that far exceed standard clean water or drywall damage. Whether triggered by a massive municipal sewage line collapse, hazardous waste contamination, animal biological infestations, or traumatic human events, biohazardous environments are loaded with millions of active bacterial colonies, bloodborne pathogens (HIV, Hepatitis B and C), Norovirus, and dangerous aerosolized toxins.

In Orlando's climate—where temperatures routinely exceed 90°F and relative humidity averages above 75%—bacterial proliferation occurs at extreme velocity. Bacteria that double every 20 minutes under ideal conditions can turn a localized spill into an airborne biohazard threat inside enclosed air-conditioned spaces within a single afternoon. Attempting to clean biological spills using household bleach, mops, or shop vacs merely aerosolizes pathogens into HVAC ducts, contaminating entire homes and commercial facilities.

Negative Pressure Containment, Sanitization & Odor Neutralization

When dealing with raw municipal backups or sewer lines pushing up through floor drains, specialists first conduct specialized sewage cleanup. Technicians isolate the affected area with zippered 6-mil polyethylene chambers and set up negative air pressure machines with HEPA filtration exhausting to the building exterior, preventing hazardous airborne bioaerosols from reaching clean rooms.

Biological decomposition produces pungent volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and cadaverine vapors that penetrate structural studs, subfloors, and ceiling joists. Dispatched contractors deploy hydroxyl generators, ozone chambers, and thermal foggers for targeted water damage odor and musty smell removal, chemically neutralizing foul odors at the molecular level rather than merely masking them with perfumes.

If liquid biohazards have saturated carpets, subflooring, or crawlspaces, crews utilize powerful truck-mounted extractors for rapid emergency water extraction before applying broad-spectrum EPA-registered virucides and fungicidal disinfectants.

The 5-Step Certified Biohazard Remediation Workflow

1

Safety Assessment, Hazard PPE & Chamber Isolation

Technicians don Level B/C personal protective equipment, including full-face respirators and chemical hazmat suits. Negative air containment barriers are erected to isolate biological pathogens from unaffected zones.

2

Removal & Selective Demolition of Porous Contaminants

Porous building materials saturated with bodily fluids or sewage (carpet padding, porous drywall, insulation) cannot be salvaged and are surgically removed, wrapped in plastic, and prepared for manifested disposal.

3

Hospital-Grade Antimicrobial & Virucidal Decontamination

Surfaces are hand-scrubbed and chemically treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants certified to eradicate Hepatitis, HIV, MRSA, C. diff spores, and pathogenic bacteria.

4

ATP Bioluminescence Testing & Clearance Verification

Rapid adenosine triphosphate (ATP) luminometer swabs test structural surfaces in real time to verify zero biological activity and confirm true sanitary clearance before containment is dismantled.

5

Licensed Biomedical Waste Transport & Manifest Filing

Contaminated materials are placed into rigid red biohazard drums, sealed, and transferred via certified biomedical waste haulers to state-licensed incineration facilities in accordance with Florida Chapter 64E-16.
