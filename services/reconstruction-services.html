Seamless Structural Rebuilding After Water & Storm Disasters

When a major plumbing rupture, slab leak, or hurricane roof tear strikes an Orlando home, the mitigation phase frequently requires aggressive selective demolition. Contaminated insulation is stripped, the bottom two to four feet of wet drywall is flood-cut away, baseboards are discarded, and water-damaged laminate or hardwood flooring is extracted down to the bare concrete slab or wooden subfloor joists.

While selective demolition stops the spread of toxic mold and bacterial growth, it leaves property owners living or working inside a gutted shell. The reconstruction phase is where craftsmanship, Florida Building Code compliance, and architectural precision matter most. Before any new materials are ordered or installed, certified teams verify that structural drying protocols have reached full equilibrium. Rebuilding prematurely over studs with moisture content above 15% traps dampness behind vapor barriers, virtually guaranteeing recurrent mold germination in Central Florida's humid climate.

Core Reconstruction Trades & Architectural Finishes

Mold-Resistant Drywall & Texture Blending

Installing fiber-reinforced, mold-resistant gypsum drywall (purple or green board). Master tapers apply flawless tape joints and feather textures—specializing in Florida knock-down, orange peel, and level-5 smooth finishes that erase all signs of flood cuts.

Waterproof Flooring & Subfloor Prep

Grinding slab micro-imperfections, applying moisture vapor barriers, and installing 100% waterproof luxury vinyl plank (LVP), porcelain tile, natural travertine, or moisture-cured engineered hardwood resilient to Florida humidity.

Custom Trim Carpentry & Millwork

Reproducing historic craftsman baseboards in Winter Park and College Park, installing matching crown molding, door casings, window sills, and resetting solid-wood interior doors swollen from moisture intrusion.

Cabinet Resetting & Countertop Fabrication

Precision leveling of custom kitchen islands, vanity replacements in flood-damaged bathrooms, and quartz/granite countertop resetting following wall framing reinforcement.

Structural Framing, Ceiling Rebuilding & Florida Code Compliance

Water leaks overhead pose unique overhead collapse hazards. When sudden plumbing failures or roof damage soak ceiling insulation, independent contractors perform complete ceiling leak water damage repair, replacing saturated acoustic drywall, securing sagging roof truss ties, and matching existing ceiling popcorn or knockdown textures without visible seam lines.

In severe structural inundations involving cracked exterior walls, termite-weakened studs exposed to moisture, or settling slab footings, teams execute comprehensive water damage restoration and structural reframing. All structural wall rebuilding adheres to the latest Florida Building Code requirements, including hurricane strapping, pressure-treated sill plates against concrete slabs, and fire-rated draft stops.

The 5-Step Post-Mitigation Rebuild Workflow

1

Moisture Clearance Verification & Scope Confirmation

A licensed restoration supervisor conducts calibrated moisture pin readings across all structural framing, concrete slabs, and insulation cavities to provide formal dry-standard clearance before reconstruction commences.

2

Xactimate Line-Item Estimating & Insurance Approval

Contractors generate a comprehensive architectural scope itemizing every sheet of drywall, linear foot of trim, and square yard of flooring to coordinate directly with your homeowners insurance adjuster.

3

Structural Framing, Electrical & Plumbing Rough-Ins

Replacing rotted wood studs, installing pressure-treated bottom plates with anti-microbial coatings, and executing permitted plumbing or electrical alterations prior to drywall closure.

4

Drywall Hanging, Taping, Texture Matching & Prime Painting

Precision-hanging mold-resistant gypsum boards, applying multi-pass mudding, feathering wall textures, and sealing with high-hide stain-blocking mold-resistant primer.

5

Flooring Installation, Trim Carpentry & Final Punch Walk

Laying new waterproof floors, resetting cabinetry and plumbing fixtures, hanging baseboards, and executing a final owner walkthrough to confirm 100% pre-loss restoration satisfaction.
