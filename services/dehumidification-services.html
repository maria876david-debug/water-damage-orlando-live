Dehumidification Questions Orlando Homeowners Ask

Why isn't my home AC enough to dry out water damage?

A residential AC is designed for comfort, not restoration. It cycles on and off based on temperature, not humidity, and typically removes far less moisture per day than commercial dehumidifiers. After water damage, you need sustained low humidity — often below 40% relative humidity — to pull moisture from inside walls and subfloors. Your AC alone can't maintain those conditions.

How long do dehumidifiers need to run after water damage?

Usually 3 to 5 days for typical Orlando water damage jobs. The equipment stays in place until moisture meters confirm that drywall, wood framing, and flooring have all reached their dry standards. In humid months, it can take a day or two longer because the dehumidifiers are fighting both the wet materials and Orlando's outdoor humidity.

Can I rent a dehumidifier instead of hiring professionals?

Rental units from hardware stores are residential-grade and undersized for real water damage. More importantly, without moisture meters you can't tell when materials are actually dry — surfaces may feel dry while the inside of the wall is still soaked. That hidden moisture leads to mold weeks later. Professional equipment plus monitoring is what prevents callbacks.

Does Orlando's humidity make dehumidification harder?

Yes, and that's exactly why professional-grade equipment matters here. When outdoor humidity sits at 80% or higher, every door opening and every air leak brings moisture back inside. Commercial dehumidifiers are sized to overcome this constant infiltration. It's also why the drying plan for an Orlando home looks different from one in Arizona — local climate shapes the approach.
