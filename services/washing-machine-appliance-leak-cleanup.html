What This Service Covers

  • Fast shutoff of high-pressure appliance water supply valves.
  • High-velocity soapy water extraction from laminate, tile, and wood.
  • Structural drying of laundry room and kitchen cabinets.
  • Baseboard removal and behind-the-wall drying.
  • Detergent residue cleaning and floor sanitizing.

Why This Happens in Orlando Homes

High municipal water pressure in many Orlando suburbs stresses rubber appliance supply hoses, causing them to crack and burst without warning. High humidity in Florida also corrodes metal connections on dishwashers and refrigerator ice-maker lines.

Our Process for Appliance Leak Cleanup

We extract soapy water, wash residues, and inject warm dry air beneath cabinets.

1

Source Isolation

We shut off the water valve behind the appliance and unplug the electrical connection to ensure a safe work area.

2

Detergent-Neutral Extraction

Standing soapy water is extracted, and we apply clean water rinses to remove sticky detergent residues from your floors.

3

Baseboard Venting

Baseboards are carefully removed, and we drill tiny, neat holes behind them to inject dry air into the wet wall cavities.

4

High-Velocity Air Circulation

Specialized air movers and LGR dehumidifiers are placed to evaporate water trapped beneath the subfloor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is water from a washing machine leak particularly damaging?

Washing machine water is classified as Category 2 "gray water" because it contains detergents, fabric softeners, and organic soil from clothing. This chemical and organic mixture is highly corrosive to subfloors and provides an ideal food source for mold if not dried immediately.

Can warped laminate flooring be saved after an appliance leak?

If we begin extraction within the first few hours of the leak, we can often save laminate flooring using specialized floor-drying mats. However, if the laminate edges have already begun to swell and split, the core material is ruined and will need replacement.

How do you dry the space underneath kitchen cabinets?

We use a specialized drying system called an injectidry, which pumps warm, dry air through small holes drilled behind the cabinet toe-kicks. This dries the dark, hidden void beneath your cabinets without requiring us to tear out your expensive kitchen cabinetry.

How can I prevent appliance leaks in my Orlando home?

We highly recommend replacing standard rubber hoses with braided stainless steel hoses, which are far more resistant to pressure spikes. You should also inspect your refrigerator water lines and dishwasher connections annually for any signs of slow corrosion.
