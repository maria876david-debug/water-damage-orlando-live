Water Heater Leak Cleanup in Orlando, FL

Urgent tank rupture and closet dry-out services.

A sudden structural failure in your water heater tank sends dozens of gallons of scalding water rushing across your utility closet floor. Saturated drywall is already wicking up the hot, rusty water, threatening to ruin your surrounding carpet and baseboards. You need professional extraction before the high heat and moisture trigger rapid mold growth.

What This Service Covers

  • Fast shutoff of the water heater intake valve and electrical supply.
  • Safe draining of the ruptured water heater tank.
  • Hot water extraction from carpets, concrete slabs, and floorboards.
  • Drywall wicking prevention and containment wall drying.
  • Rust stain removal and antimicrobial wash of closet surfaces.

Why This Happens in Orlando Homes

Orlando's hard groundwater is packed with calcium and minerals that accumulate at the bottom of heating tanks over time. This sediment buildup, combined with constant thermal expansion in Florida's warm climate, corrodes the inner tank lining until it ruptures.

Our Process for Water Heater Cleanup

We use target drying systems to completely dry small closets and adjacent floorboards.

1

Energy & Water Isolation

We safely shut down the unit's electrical breaker or gas supply, shut off the incoming water supply, and drain the remaining water.

2

Scalding Water Extraction

Saturated surfaces are vacuumed using high-temperature-tolerant extraction tools to remove the hot, mineral-heavy water.

3

Containment & Baseboard Removal

We carefully remove vinyl baseboards to allow trapped moisture behind the utility closet walls to escape.

4

Targeted Drying Matrix

Commercial air movers and LGR dehumidifiers are focused directly inside the closet to dry the drywall cavities and subfloor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Speak to a Technician
How fast does mold grow after a hot water heater leak?

Mold grows exceptionally fast after a water heater leak because the water is warm, which creates a perfect incubation environment when combined with Orlando's high humidity. Spores can begin germinating within twelve to eighteen hours on damp drywall, making immediate extraction and drying critical.

What causes a water heater tank to suddenly burst?

Over time, mineral deposits and sediment settle at the bottom of the tank, acting as an insulating barrier that causes the burner or heating elements to overheat. This constant overheating weakens the steel tank wall and causes microscopic cracks that eventually fail under pressure.

Can you extract water from the carpet outside the water heater closet?

Yes, we use heavy-duty weighted extraction tools to pull water out of the carpet and padding without removing the carpet if possible. If the water was rusty, we apply specialized browning spotters to remove rust stains and ensure your carpet looks pristine.

Will my insurance pay for a new water heater and the cleanup?

While homeowners insurance typically covers the water damage cleanup, structural drying, and flooring repairs caused by the leak, it usually does not cover the cost of buying a new water heater unit itself, as that is considered standard appliance maintenance.
