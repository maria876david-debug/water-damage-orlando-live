Deep subsurface carpet and padding dewatering.

What This Service Covers

  • Deep subsurface water extraction using weighted extraction rovers.
  • Carpet pad inspection, drying, or eco-friendly replacement.
  • Subfloor drying (plywood, concrete, or hardwood) beneath the carpet.
  • Carpet stretching, reinstalling, and professional tack strip repair.
  • Heavy-duty deodorizing and antimicrobial fiber treatment.

Why This Happens in Orlando Homes

High indoor humidity in Orlando homes caused by failing AC systems prevents damp carpets from drying naturally. This allows minor spills or pipe leaks to stay trapped in the carpet pad, causing rapid microbial growth.

Our Process for Carpet Water Extraction

We squeeze water deep from carpet pad and dry the subfloor before stretching fibers back.

1

Subsurface Extraction

We use heavy, ride-on extraction rovers that apply deep pressure to squeeze water out of both the carpet fibers and the dense padding below.

2

Floating the Carpet

If needed, we carefully un-tack the carpet edges and blow warm, dry air directly beneath the carpet to dry the pad and subfloor.

3

Antimicrobial Sanitizing

We apply an EPA-registered sanitizer to both sides of the carpet to kill odor-causing bacteria and mold spores.

4

Stretching & Re-installation

Once fully dry, we stretch the carpet back to its original shape and secure it onto new tack strips.

Can a wet carpet pad be saved, or does it have to be replaced?

If the water was clean Category 1 water (like a plumbing leak) and we begin extraction within twenty-four hours, we can easily save both the carpet and pad. However, if the water was dirty or remained wet for over forty-eight hours, the pad must be replaced.

What causes a carpet to smell musty after water damage?

The musty smell is caused by microscopic mold and mildew spores that feed on dust, hair, and organic debris trapped deep inside the carpet backing and pad. When water is added, these spores activate and multiply, releasing volatile organic compounds into the air.

What is "carpet delamination" and how do you prevent it?

Carpet delamination occurs when the secondary backing of the carpet separates from the primary backing because the adhesive has dissolved in water. To prevent this, we avoid heavy foot traffic on wet carpet and dry it rapidly using professional subsurface tools.

How long does it take for a carpet to dry completely?

With our professional truck-mounted extraction and high-velocity air movers, carpets typically dry within twenty-four to thirty-six hours. We use moisture meters with specialized pins to verify that the subfloor beneath the carpet is also completely dry.
