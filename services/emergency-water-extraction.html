Emergency Water Extraction Questions

What should I do first when I find flooding in my Orlando home?

First, stop the source if you safely can — shut off the main water valve for pipe bursts, or turn off the appliance. Then shut off electricity to affected areas if water is near outlets. Move valuables to dry ground, take photos for insurance, and call for professional extraction. Don't wait to see if it dries on its own; in Orlando's humidity, it won't.

How fast does water damage get worse?

Fast. Water wicks up drywall at roughly an inch per hour. Within 24 hours, you risk mold growth, wood swelling, and delamination. By 48-72 hours in Florida's climate, mold is likely established and materials may need removal instead of drying. Every hour of standing water increases the final restoration cost.

Can I use a wet vacuum instead of professional extraction?

A shop vac helps with surface water but can't match truck-mounted extraction, which pulls water from carpet padding and subfloors. More importantly, DIY extraction doesn't include moisture detection — you won't know if water got behind baseboards or under flooring. For anything beyond a small spill, professional extraction prevents the hidden damage that causes mold later.

What happens after the water is extracted?

Extraction is step one. After that comes moisture mapping to find hidden wet areas, strategic placement of air movers and dehumidifiers, daily monitoring until everything reaches dry standards, and antimicrobial treatment where needed. The full process typically takes 3-5 days for a standard residential job in Orlando.
