Water mitigation is the immediate emergency phase aimed at stopping active water intrusion, extracting standing liquid, and deploying industrial dehumidifiers to prevent mold growth. Water restoration follows mitigation and involves repairing structural components, installing new drywall, replacing damaged flooring, and returning the property to its pre-loss condition.
No, we provide a free initial on-site moisture assessment for property owners in Orlando and Orange County. Our certified technicians use thermal imaging and non-invasive moisture meters to determine the exact perimeter of water penetration before presenting a scope of work.
Household box fans only circulate humid ambient air across wet surfaces without extracting moisture from the room. In Florida's high humidity, surface-level fan drying leaves moisture trapped inside wall studs and subfloors, causing mold colonies to germinate. Professional low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers extract dozens of pints of airborne water per day to physically pull moisture out of dense materials.
Chlorine bleach is 90% water and cannot penetrate porous substrates like drywall or unsealed framing timber. While bleach temporarily removes surface pigmentation, the moisture feeds the deep fungal root network. Permanent remediation requires removing contaminated porous material and maintaining structural humidity below 50%.
