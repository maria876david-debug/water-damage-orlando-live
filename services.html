Standard Operating Procedure

Our 5-Stage Scientific Restoration Process

Water damage mitigation cannot be guessed. Connected contractors apply strict psychrometric formulas to calculate exact airflow and moisture extraction requirements for every room.

1
Immediate Intake

Phone Triage & Rapid Field Dispatch

You speak with our intake team to assess the water damage category (Clean, Gray, or Black water) and get connected with an independent local emergency crew dispatched with typical arrival within 45 to 60 minutes*.

2
Diagnostic Mapping

Thermal Imaging & Moisture Penetration Mapping

FLIR thermal cameras and non-penetrating RF moisture meters track hidden water trails behind baseboards, under tiled slabs, and within ceiling drywall without causing destructive wall damage.

3
Bulk Removal

Industrial Extraction & Sanitization

Truck-mounted extractors pull bulk standing water from carpets, hardwood, and concrete. Crews apply EPA-registered botanical antimicrobials to neutralize bacteria and halt microbial colonization on contact.

4
Psychrometric Control

Structural Dehumidification & Daily Monitoring

Commercial low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers run continuously. Field techs return daily to record grain depression (GPP) until moisture levels match baseline dry standards.

5
Final Closeout

Structural Rebuild & Insurance Documentation

Contractors supply dry certificates, itemized Xactimate loss logs, and photo documentation to your insurance adjuster, ensuring complete reimbursement clarity while construction teams restore damaged finishes.

Water mitigation is the immediate emergency phase aimed at stopping active water intrusion, extracting standing liquid, and deploying industrial dehumidifiers to prevent mold growth. Water restoration follows mitigation and involves repairing or rebuilding structural components, installing new drywall, replacing damaged flooring, and returning the property to its pre-loss condition.

Household fans only move surface air and do not extract water vapor from enclosed spaces. In Central Florida's ambient humidity, blowing moist air around without commercial low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers often accelerates mold spore dispersal while leaving deep structural framing saturated. Professional extractors and dehumidifiers are required to pull trapped water out of porous materials.
Spraying surface cleaners on porous drywall or timber only addresses the visible surface layer while the root hyphae remain alive deep inside the damp substrate. Chlorine bleach cannot penetrate porous cellulose and actually introduces water that feeds remaining spores. Permanent eradication requires mechanical extraction, negative-air containment, and structural drying to below standard moisture thresholds.
