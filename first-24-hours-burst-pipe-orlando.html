What to Do in the First 24 Hours After a Burst Pipe

Discover critical action steps to take immediately after a pipe bursts in your Orlando home to stop structural damage and prevent mold growth.

O Orlando Rapid Restoration
·June 2026 ·7 min read

A ruptured plumbing line can dump hundreds of gallons of pressurized water across your living space in a matter of minutes. When a pipe fails inside your home, every hour that passes allows moisture to seep deeper into drywall, baseboards, and flooring support beams.

Stop the Flow and Secure the Property Immediately

Your very first move is to shut off the main water supply valve to halt the flooding. In most Central Florida properties, this valve sits outside near the street meter or on the exterior side wall of the garage. If the turn valve is rusted or stuck, use a water meter key or adjustable wrench to turn it clockwise until the water stops. If you live in a multistory condo building in Downtown Orlando or a shared residential complex in Lake Nona, contact your property manager or building maintenance team right away to cut the main supply line.

Once the water stops flowing, prioritize personal safety before stepping into flooded rooms. Turn off the circuit breakers for any affected areas to eliminate electrical hazards. If water has reached overhead light fixtures or electrical outlets, keep your family out of the room until an electrician or certified technician confirms the space is secure.

Relocate valuable furniture, electronics, and important documents to dry ground. In historic properties across Winter Park and College Park, wooden heirloom furniture can absorb water rapidly from saturated rugs. Place aluminum foil squares or wood blocks under furniture legs if you cannot lift heavy pieces out of the wet room.

Document the Destruction for Your Insurance Claim

Before you begin cleaning up standing water, take thorough photographs and video footage of the entire scene. Insurance adjusters need clear visual evidence of the standing water depth and the ruined personal belongings to process your claim accurately.

Capture wide angle photos of affected rooms along with close up shots of damaged plumbing fixtures, saturated carpets, and peeling paint. Walk through every flooded space, from kitchen pantries to hallway closets, so nothing gets missed during your initial report.

Do not throw away ruined materials like soaked drywall chunks, saturated ceiling tiles, or damaged carpeting until your insurance representative gives you explicit approval. Keeping physical samples or thorough photographic records prevents disputes over the severity of the incident when your field adjuster inspects the property. If you must remove wet rugs or ruined furniture to clear a safe walking path, document their exact condition and serial numbers first.

Why Rapid Water Extraction Is Essential in Central Florida

Orlando weather brings warm temperatures and high humidity that make indoor water accidents especially hazardous for local homeowners. In neighborhoods like the Mills 50 District and College Park, trapped moisture inside closed wall cavities will begin breeding mold spores within 24 to 48 hours.

You can use clean towels and heavy duty buckets for minor surface spills, but a major plumbing failure requires commercial extraction equipment. Standard shop vacuums simply cannot pull water from deep carpet padding, tile underlayment, or hardwood flooring grooves. Our field teams use truck mounted extraction units and weighted subfloor tools to draw moisture out of dense construction materials without destroying the floorboards. For extensive residential flooding incidents, scheduling water damage restoration ensures that every gallon of hidden water is removed before structural decay sets in.

Removing liquid water is only the first phase of the structural recovery process. Once the standing water is gone, high velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers must run continuously to capture evaporation and bring indoor relative humidity back below normal levels. If your property is facing standing water right now, professional emergency water extraction will stop the absorption process before flooring framing begins to rot.

Essential First Day Restoration Steps

To keep your recovery on track during those critical initial hours, follow a structured checklist. Taking orderly action prevents secondary structural damage and protects your property value.

  • Shut off the main water valve and disable electrical circuits in all flooded zones.
  • Record detailed photos and videos of all standing water and ruined personal items.
  • Remove liquid water using professional grade pumps or extraction vacuums.
  • Open interior doors, closets, and cabinet drawers to increase indoor air circulation.
  • Contact an IICRC certified restoration team to begin professional structural drying.

Acting quickly on these steps minimizes the chances of drywall sagging and hardwood buckling. It also ensures your living environment remains safe for your family while permanent repairs get underway.

When to Call Professional Restoration Specialists

Attempting to dry a flooded house without commercial moisture detection equipment leaves hidden pockets of water trapped behind baseboards, under vinyl floor coverings, and inside ceiling cavities. Since 2008, our crew has responded to plumbing emergencies across Orlando, and we regularly see severe mold outbreaks caused by incomplete do it yourself drying attempts. Standard box fans only move wet air around the room without actually removing moisture from the structure.

Certified technicians use thermal imaging cameras and noninvasive moisture meters to map exact water migration patterns inside your walls and ceilings. This scientific approach guarantees that every square inch of your framing and insulation is completely dry before reconstruction begins.
